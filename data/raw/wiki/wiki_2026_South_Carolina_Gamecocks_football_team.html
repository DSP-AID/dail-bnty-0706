The 2026 South Carolina Gamecocks football team represents the University of South Carolina as a member of the Southeastern Conference (SEC) during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The Gamecocks play their home games at Williams–Brice Stadium located in Columbia, South Carolina, and are led by Shane Beamer in his sixth year as their head coach.

Despite a confounding 0-3 start to conference play and speculation about Beamer's job security, South Carolina mounted an enormous mid-season turnaround, spearheaded by back to back victories over top 15 ranked teams, #16 Florida and #11 Tennessee.[1] In beating Florida on the road, Shane Beamer became the only coach in program history besides Steve Spurrier to beat the Gators in the Swamp.[2] Carolina's upset win at home against #11 Tennessee resulted in a field storming at Williams–Brice Stadium, the third of Beamer's tenure. The following week, the Gamecocks traveled to Norman, Oklahoma, and trounced the Oklahoma Sooners, featuring one of the country's toughest defenses, by a score of 42-25. South Carolina extended their conference winning streak to four straight after beating the Texas A&M Aggies at home in a gritty, low-scoring game, solidifying their status of the hottest teams in the country, and star quarterback LaNorris Sellers' penchant for theatrical heroics.

[edit]
See also: 2026 NFL draft

The deadline for players to declare for the NFL draft was January 14, 2026.

Three South Carolina players were drafted in 2026.

During the second round of the draft that April, cornerback Brandon Cisse was the 52nd pick overall, selected by the Green Bay Packers; in the fifth round, defensive tackle Nick Barrett (#145) was selected by the Los Angeles Chargers; safety Jalon Kilgore (#167) was selected by the Buffalo Bills.
