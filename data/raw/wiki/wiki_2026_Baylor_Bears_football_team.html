The 2026 Baylor Bears football team represents Baylor University as a member of the Big 12 Conference during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The Bears play home games at McLane Stadium located in Waco, Texas.

The Bears were led by Dave Aranda in his seventh year as their head coach for the first seven games of the season. Aranda was fired on October 25, finishing at Baylor with an overall record of 40–40; defensive coordinator Joe Klanderman was named the team's interim head coach for the remainder of the season.[1]
