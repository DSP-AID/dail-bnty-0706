The 2026 San Diego State Aztecs football team represents San Diego State University as a member of the Pac-12 Conference during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The team is led by third-year head coach Sean Lewis, and play their home games at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego.

This is the Aztecs' first season in the Pac-12, after they were a member of the Mountain West for most sports since 1999.[1]

Offseason

[edit]

On December 7, 2025, defensive coordinator Rob Aurich was hired to the same position at Nebraska.[2] The next day, cornerbacks coach Demetrius Sumler was named the interim defensive coordinator for the New Mexico Bowl.[3] On December 20, a week ahead of the bowl game, Sumler was announced as the team's permanent defensive coordinator.[4]

Preseason

[edit]

The Aztecs started their offseason with nine weeks of winter workouts, starting on January 23.[1] Spring practice began on March 23, which is scheduled to culminate with the spring game on May 2 at the SDSU practice fields.[5][6]
