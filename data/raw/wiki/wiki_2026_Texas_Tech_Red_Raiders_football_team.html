The 2026 Texas Tech Red Raiders football team represents Texas Tech University during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season as a member of the Big 12 Conference. The Red Raiders are led by fifth-year head coach Joey McGuire and play home games at Galaxy Stadium in Lubbock, Texas.[1] The Red Raiders entered the 2026 season as the defending Big 12 champions.[2]
