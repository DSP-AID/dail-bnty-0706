The 2026 Maryland Terrapins football team represents the University of Maryland, College Park in the Big Ten Conference during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The Terrapins play their home games at SECU Stadium located in College Park, Maryland.

The Terrapins were led by eighth year head coach Mike Locksley for the first seven games of the season. Locksley was fired on October 21 with co-defensive coordinator Aazaar Abdul-Rahim being named interim head coach.[2]
