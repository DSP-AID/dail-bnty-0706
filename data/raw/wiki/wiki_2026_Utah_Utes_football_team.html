The 2026 Utah Utes football team is an American college football team that represents the University of Utah as a member of the Big 12 Conference during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. Morgan Scalley was promoted to head coach after Kyle Whittingham's departure in December 2025 to become head coach at Michigan.[1] In their first year under Scalley, and with two games remaining in the regular season, the Utes have compiled an undefeated 10–0 record (7–0 in conference games) and are ranked No. 2 in the AP and Coaches polls. In two games against ranked opponents, the Utes defeated No. 9 BYU and No. 23 Arizona.

Through the first ten games, the team's statistical leaders are quarterback Devon Dampier (2,257 passing yards), running back Wayshawn Parker (764 rushing yards, 78 points scored), wide receiver Braden Pegan (47 receptions for 643 yards), and linebacker Trey Reynolds (60 total tackles).[2]

The team plays its home games at Rice–Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City, Utah.
