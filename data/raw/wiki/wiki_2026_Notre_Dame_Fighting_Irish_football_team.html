The 2026 Notre Dame Fighting Irish football team represents the University of Notre Dame as an independent during the 2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season. The Fighting Irish are led by fifth-year head coach Marcus Freeman. They play their home games at Notre Dame Stadium located in Notre Dame, Indiana.

In the final play of the game, Notre Dame quarterback CJ Carr threw an incomplete pass intended for wide receiver Jaden Greathouse that was broken up by BYU cornerback Evan Johnson. Controversy arose when many analysts believed that Johnson should have been penalized for pass interference, as well as multiple shots showing the stadium clock having one second left when the ball touched the ground. After the game, the Big 12 Conference stated that the game clock had one second left when the game was declared over and that the play was incorrectly cleared by the replay officiating crew. Had the game not been declared over, Notre Dame would have had another attempt to score from BYU's 8-yard line. The game's ending has been compared to Michigan's controversial win over Western Michigan in September 2026.[9][10]
